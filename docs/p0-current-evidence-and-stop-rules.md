**Source/native/synthetic are different kinds of evidence.** A source-verified instruction or syntactically reachable call is not an executed gameplay event. Two arrays with equal float-like words are not calibrated physical coordinates. Human labels such as “airborne”, “apex” and “landed” on the seven old dumps are **not independently phase-verified**. Neither synthetic CI nor 14-bank hashes proves same-boot or same-game-step relationships among the captures. Successful user scripts may prove specific source predicates without proving the global P0.
