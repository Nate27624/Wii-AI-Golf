## Research question

Can public, source-gated phase-labelled RAM summaries reveal repeatable non-rendering memory changes that discriminate club-related behavior from idle and aim/camera controls, before claiming a measured swing-power variable?

**H0:** changing region-relative memory words are unrelated to golfer action or are explainable by idle/camera/scene effects. **H1 (exploratory):** some source-grounded candidate changes recur independently and are enriched in club-motion/impact stages relative to matched idle and camera-only stages. H1 cannot be tested rigorously from current aggregate S002 summary because it lacks per-offset, per-phase change counts and same-shot provenance; this study identifies and reports that blocker rather than claiming discovery.

Current historical evidence: V1 speed-only 0.8 and 0.9 plus backswing-only 0.8 yielded human-observed ball contacts with approximately 75% **visual estimates** of power, off-green display 100.0 ft. That is a penalty, **not physical carry**. The S002 minimap rendering offsets and source-documented configuration/field manager/sequence structures are not decoded golfer state. `RPGolScene` source has an `mGolMain*` member at scene-instance offset `+0x9C`, but the live scene address and pointed-to layout are NOT established. Never dereference guesses.
